A little about me ♡

“Flour on the apron, joy in the kitchen.”

Ofelia in the studio
Greetings from my baking bench

“Hi, I'm Ofelia! I'm a pastry baker who loves exploring the little details behind every sweet creation. Through my learning journey, I get to discover new techniques, experiment with flavors, and turn simple ingredients into something special.”

What started as a childhood fascination with Sunday tarts has grown into a devoted studio practice. I focus on classical foundations paired with gentle, nostalgic aesthetics — finding poetry in butter temperatures, precision crumb structures, and quiet morning piping sessions.

temp_preferences_custom

My Pastry Philosophy

Patience over haste. Respecting the dough's natural resting rhythm and letting authentic flavors speak louder than excessive sugar.

eco

Favorite Ingredients

Single-origin Tahitian vanilla bean, slow-cooked hazelnut brown butter, organic citrus zest, and flaky Maldon sea salt.

♡
Why I Bake: For the quiet joy of sharing handmade sweetness with friends, family, and kind strangers.

My Learning Journey

Every lesson, every creation, every little progress.

MODULE 01 The Foundations

Basic Baking

Understanding ingredients, measurements, and baking fundamentals. Demystifying the chemistry of hydration percentages, butter aeration, and oven heat dynamics.

Key Techniques Mastered:
  • ♡ Gram-scale precision weighing
  • ♡ Ambient temperature control
  • ♡ Leavening agents balancing
MODULE 02 Wild Fermentation

Bread Making

Exploring dough preparation, kneading, and fermentation. Learning to read dough elasticity by touch rather than just the kitchen timer.

Key Techniques Mastered:
  • ♡ 2-Hour autolyse method
  • ♡ Translucent windowpane test
  • ♡ Boule & batard bench tension
MODULE 03 Decorative Artistry

Cake Decoration

Learning cake finishing, piping, and decorative techniques. Transforming sponges into nostalgic pieces of confectionery art inspired by European tea salons.

Key Techniques Mastered:
  • ♡ Silky Swiss meringue buttercream
  • ♡ Palette knife floral texture
  • ♡ Hand-tied satin ribbon finishing
MODULE 04 Lamination & Custards

Pastry Fundamentals

Discovering pastry textures, layering, and preparation methods. Mastering fragile pâte à choux expansions, crisp blind-baked tarts, and buttery viennoiserie.

Key Techniques Mastered:
  • ♡ Tourage butter lamination
  • ♡ Pâte à choux steam elevation
  • ♡ Blind-baking sweet shortcrust
MODULE 05 Daily Synthesis

My Practice

A collection of personal practice, flavor trial notebooks, and learning outcomes. Testing seasonal preserves and documenting failure-to-success milestones.

Key Techniques Mastered:
  • ♡ Daily small-batch micro testing
  • ♡ Herbal & fruit botanical pairings
  • ♡ Iterative crumb texture journaling

Little Skills, Big Progress

Baking Fundamentals

Kitchen Chemistry

Mastering baker percentages, thermal equilibrium of butter, moisture retention in crumb, and flour protein content adjustments for varying crumb tenderness.

♡ Daily precision measurement habit
bakery_dining

Dough Preparation

Yeast & Sourdough

Manual slap-and-fold techniques, autolyse hydration, tracking fermentation by gas volume, and applying gentle surface tension during final shaping.

♡ Tactile elasticity intuition
brush

Cake Decorating

Lambeth & Florals

Intricate shell borders, scrollwork, garland drops with #16 & #18 star tips, mirror glazes, and balancing pastel color gradients with botanical garnishes.

♡ Steady hand & calm pacing

Pastry Techniques

Lamination & Choux

Enclosing European butter blocks (beurrage), executing single and double turns without butter fracture, and baking steam-hollowed pâte à choux.
